My Research

Environmental scientist with expertise in land degradation assessment, remote sensing, and Mediterranean ecosystems. My research focuses on understanding and monitoring environmental changes using satellite data and participatory approaches.

Research Areas

  • Remote Sensing - NDVI analysis and satellite data interpretation
  • Environmental Data Science - Machine learning applications for sustainability
  • Land Degradation Assessment - Using remote sensing and landscape approaches
  • Mediterranean Ecosystems - Forest resilience and rangeland management
  • Participatory Assessment - Community-based environmental monitoring

Publications

Peer-Reviewed Articles

How does land management contribute to the resilience of Mediterranean forests and rangelands? A participatory assessment

Authors: Jucker Riva, M., Baeza, J., Bautista, S., Christoforou, M., Daliakopoulos, I.N., Hadjimitsis, D., Keizer, J.J., Liniger, H., Quaranta, G., Ribeiro, C., Salvia, R., Tsanis, I.K., Urgeghe, A.M., Valdecantos, A., Schwilch, G.

Journal: Land Degradation & Development, 29(10), 3721-3735 (2018)

DOI: 10.1002/ldr.3104

Abstract: In Mediterranean forests and rangelands, the supply of important ecosystem services can decrease or cease as a consequence of disturbances and climatic oscillations. Land managers can sometimes prevent or mitigate the negative effects of disturbances through appropriate land management choices.

Assessment of land degradation in Mediterranean forests and grazing lands using a landscape unit approach and the normalized difference vegetation index

Authors: Jucker Riva, M., Daliakopoulos, I.N., Eckert, S., Hodel, E., Liniger, H.

Journal: Applied Geography, 86, 8-21 (2017)

DOI: 10.1016/j.apgeog.2017.06.017

Abstract: Land managers need sound, evidence-based information about land degradation patterns and about the effectiveness of their management responses. This study presents a landscape unit approach combined with NDVI analysis for assessing land degradation in Mediterranean environments.

Impacts of Land Management on the Resilience of Mediterranean Dry Forests to Fire

Authors: Jucker Riva, M., Liniger, H., Valdecantos, A., Schwilch, G.

Journal: Sustainability, 8(10), 981 (2016)

DOI: 10.3390/su8100981

Abstract: Wildfires have always been a part of the history of Mediterranean forests. However, forests are not always certain to regenerate after a wildfire. This study investigates how land management practices can increase forest resilience to fire through regeneration mechanisms.
